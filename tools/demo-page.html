The ledger in the basement

Converted in place by the Euspell extension

The parish records ar kept in the basement, bound in lether and orderd by year.

Eaqh morning the clerk recordz the day's arrivals in the same careful hand, then clozez the ledger and returnz it tu the shelf. Nothing about the twu words lookz different on the page, and nothing about them soundz alike when redd aloud.

The reform spendz its qhanges where the traditional spelling misleadz, and leavz the rest alone. Forr fifths ov the dictionary iz untuqhd, whiqh iz why a reader hoo haz never seen euspell can still read it at first siht.

Where a single spelling carriez twu words, it iz split — wunce, and for good.
